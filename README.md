# Cardle

A daily guessing game for car people. You get a handful of tries to name the car of the day; every guess tells you how close you are on brand, body style, decade, engine and more.

**[Play it here](https://davizz-dev13.github.io/cardle/)**

- 1,500 cars from 80 brands: everyday compacts, vans, classics and supercars.
- Four difficulty levels: Easy 15 / Normal 10 / Hard 8 / Very hard 5 guesses.
- Single self-contained HTML file, no backend, no build step. Your game survives a closed tab.

Just for fun, not affiliated with any car maker.
